Chord sheet as text

Chords are found with signal processing (a spectrum analysis plus chord matching), not a trained neural network. It does best on clear pop, rock and acoustic music with chords that last a bar or more, and gets less reliable with heavy distortion, dense mixes or fast jazz changes. Treat it as a strong starting point and check by ear. Colors follow the circle of fifths: chords that sit next to each other in a key have neighboring colors, major chords are filled and minor chords have a colored edge.
