Chord sheet as text

This listens with signal processing (a spectrum analysis plus chord matching), not a trained neural network. It does best on clear pop, rock and acoustic music with chords that last a bar or more, and gets less reliable with heavy distortion, dense mixes, or rapid jazz changes. Treat it as a strong starting point and check it by ear.
